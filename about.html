About the OGITECH Past Questions Bank

A student-focused academic resource created to make past examination questions easier to find and use for revision at the Ogun State Institute of Technology, Igbesa.

01 / Project history

How the project came about

The OGITECH Past Questions Bank was developed as a digital academic resource to address a practical challenge faced by students: finding relevant past examination questions for their courses. Instead of relying only on scattered copies and informal sharing, the platform provides a central place where students can browse available materials by department, programme, level, semester, course and examination year.

The project brings together a public website and an administrative system. The public side allows students to discover, preview and download published materials, while authorised administrators organise academic information, manage question submissions and respond to requests for missing materials.

The platform is intended to grow with the institution's academic structure and to preserve useful learning resources for successive groups of students.

02 / The institution

About Ogun State Institute of Technology

Ogun State Institute of Technology (OGITECH) is a tertiary institution located in Igbesa, Ogun State, Nigeria. Its academic community includes students, lecturers, non-teaching staff, institutional management and the Students' Union Government.

This platform is built around the academic needs of that community. Its department, programme, level, semester and academic-session information is maintained within the application so that the resources presented to students can reflect the institution's approved academic structure.

This page records the institution's identity and the context in which the project exists. For official institutional history, policies and announcements, readers should consult OGITECH's authorised publications and communication channels.

03 / Purpose

Why the Past Questions Bank exists

  • Make available past examination questions easier to discover.
  • Organise resources around the institution's academic structure.
  • Give students a straightforward way to preview and download published materials.
  • Allow students to request questions that are not yet available.
  • Support independent revision alongside lectures, notes and other approved learning materials.
  • Preserve useful academic resources for future students.
